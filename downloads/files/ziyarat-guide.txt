NAGORE DARGAH SHARIFF
A Simple Guide to Ziyarat
=========================

1. Arrive with purity of intention (niyyat) and a calm heart.
2. Observe modest dress and remove footwear before entering.
3. Proceed through the main gateway toward the Roza (holy tomb).
4. Offer salaam and Fathiha at the shrine of Nagore Andavar (Q.S.).
5. Make your dua; vows (niyyat) may be offered with sincerity.
6. Visitors of all faiths are welcomed with respect.

Darshan is open daily from early morning to late night.
Timings may extend during the Uroos festival.
