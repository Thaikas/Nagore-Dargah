Kanduri Uroos Mubarak — Indicative Calendar
==========================================

The Uroos commemorates the death anniversary (Urs) of
Hazrat Syed Shahul Hameed Qadir Wali (Q.S.) over fourteen days.
Exact dates follow the Islamic lunar calendar and are announced
each year by the Dargah administration.

Day 1        Kodiyetram (Flag Hoisting) — opening of the Uroos
Days 2-6     Qiraat, Zikr and Qawwali assemblies
Days 7-10    Niyyat, Fathiha and Annadhanam (community feeding)
Days 11-13   Sandal anointment ceremonies
Day 14       Santhanakoodu (Sandalwood procession) — grand finale
